Implementation Blueprint

This blueprint provides a practical architecture to deliver a secure and scalable School Management System.

Module Layers

  • Identity & access management (RBAC)
  • Academics (classes, attendance, exams, results)
  • Finance (fees, gateway, receipts)
  • Communication (notices, email/SMS, messages)
  • Administration (logs, backups, settings)

Delivery Roadmap

  • Phase 1: Authentication + role dashboards
  • Phase 2: Attendance, timetable, assignment modules
  • Phase 3: Exams, results, fee system, reports
  • Phase 4: Analytics, backup/restore, and optimization
Built for digital school transformation.
